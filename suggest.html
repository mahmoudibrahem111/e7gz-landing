What is E7GZ?

E7GZ connects venue owners with players — players find and book a court in seconds, and owners run their court, bookings, and revenue from one place.

For players
  • Find nearby courts with photos, prices and ratings.
  • See free slots and book online in seconds.
  • Pay online and track all your bookings in the app.
  • Create or join a match when you need players.
For venue owners
  • List your court and get new bookings instead of calls.
  • Set prices and hours — block any slot from online booking.
  • See your calendar, free slots, revenue and booking notifications.
  • Manage everything in one place — no paper or WhatsApp.
