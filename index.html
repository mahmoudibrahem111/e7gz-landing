FEATURES

Everything you need
to enjoy the game

Whether you play or you run the venue, E7GZ gives you all the tools — book fields, join matches, manage your game experience, and run your business.

Discover & Filter Venues

Browse stadiums near you on a map, filter by pitch type — 5-a-side, 7-a-side, indoor futsal — compare prices, and save favorites.

Instant Slot Booking

Pick a venue, see real-time availability with live prices in EGP, choose your slot, and pay securely with cards or mobile wallets via Paymob.

Join or Host Matches

Find open matches near you, see spots filled in real time, join with one tap — or create your own match and chat with your squad.

Track Your Bookings

All your reservations in one place — upcoming, past, and cancelled — with venue details, times, prices, and full booking history.

Live Owner Dashboard

Today's revenue, bookings, and free slots at a glance — across all your venues. Switch between facilities instantly and record walk-in customers.

Daily Calendar

Every booking on a clear timeline. See bookings, revenue, and rate per hour for any day, and manage confirmed slots for each venue.

Weekly Hours Template

Set recurring opening hours once — future slots generate automatically. Toggle days on or off, adjust open and close times, and override any single day later.

Multi-Venue Management

Register new venues, list photos, prices, and amenities, and manage all your facilities from one app — with walk-in bookings to keep everything accurate.
