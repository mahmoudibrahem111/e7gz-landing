Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 — Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how E7GZ (the "App", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects the personal data of people who use the App, including players who book stadiums and join matches, and stadium owners who list their venues.

The App is operated by Mahmoud Ibrahim, an individual. E7GZ is the name of the project and is not a registered company.

By using the App or agreeing to this Policy when you register, you acknowledge that you have read it. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Definitions

  • "App": The E7GZ application, its interfaces, and related services.
  • "Personal Data": Information that can be linked to a specific user or that helps identify them.
  • "Player": A user who books stadiums or joins matches.
  • "Owner": A user who lists and manages stadiums.
  • "Booking": A reservation of a time slot at a stadium made through the App.
  • "Match": A sporting event created or joined through the App.
  • "Stadium": A stadium, pitch, court, or similar sports facility listed on the App.

2. Data Controller

The App is operated, and personal data is controlled, by Mahmoud Ibrahim, an individual. E7GZ is a project and trade name used for the App and is not a registered company.

Contact: e7gzsupport@gmail.com

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account and Profile Data

We may collect your first name, last name, username, email address, phone number, password, and any additional details you choose to add to your profile. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are not stored in plain text.

3.2 Verification Data

We use one-time passcodes (OTPs), currently sent by email, to verify accounts. We may record that a verification attempt took place, when it occurred, whether it succeeded, and the channel used.

3.3 Booking Records

We collect booking data, including the stadium, time slot, price, booking status, cancellation status, refund status, and the times of any changes made to the booking. This data is used to manage bookings, serve users, handle disputes, and maintain financial records.

3.4 Payments

Booking payments are currently made directly between the Player and the Owner using InstaPay. E7GZ does not receive, hold, or process this money.

When you pay by InstaPay, you upload a screenshot of your transfer together with the transfer reference, and the Owner uploads their own receipt and confirms the Booking. We store these images and the reference in private file storage. We may process the uploaded screenshot automatically to read the reference number (text extraction, also known as OCR) to help fill in the details.

We do not store bank card numbers. Card and e-wallet payments are not currently available in the App. If we make them available in the future, they will be processed by a third-party payment provider, and we will keep only transaction-related data such as a transaction or reference number, the amount, the payment method, and the date.

3.5 Location

If you grant the App permission to access your device's location, we may use it to show nearby stadiums, rank search results, and display directions. Location permission is optional; you can search by city, area, or name without it. Maps, geocoding, and directions rely on Google Maps services, which may process data in accordance with their own policies and terms.

3.6 Matches and Social Features

When you create or join a match, we process your participation data, player lists, invitations, and the details you provide. Other match participants may be able to see your username and certain profile information made available to them within the App.

3.7 Chats and Reports

We process the messages you send in match chats and direct chats, as well as reports you submit about users or content. This data is used to provide the messaging service, handle reports, address abuse, and maintain the safety of the service. Messages are stored in our database and are not routinely reviewed, but they may be accessed when necessary to handle a report, a security issue, abuse, or a legal request.

3.8 Stadium Owner Data

If you register as an Owner, we may process data about the stadiums you list, including names, addresses, locations, photos, prices, schedules, descriptions, and facilities. We may also process the review status of a stadium and any direct (walk-in) bookings you record.

3.9 Server and Technical Data

Our servers automatically log certain technical data associated with your use of the App, such as your IP address, App version, device and operating system information, and errors. We use error-monitoring tools to detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and faults. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not use your data for behavioral advertising.

3.10 Notifications and App Version

When you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can deliver booking, match, and account notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings. When the App starts, it checks with our servers whether a newer version is required and which version you are running; this is used to keep the App secure and up to date.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Under Egyptian law, including the Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, we process your personal data on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to create and manage your account, process bookings and payments, provide chat and notifications, and deliver the features you request.
  • Consent: where you agree, for example when you accept these policies during sign-up, grant location permission, or enable notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the App, prevent fraud and abuse, and defend legal claims.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable Egyptian laws and respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.

5. How We Use Data

We use the data we collect to create, operate, and secure accounts; verify accounts; display stadiums and manage bookings, cancellations, and refunds; operate matches, invitations, and in-app notifications; provide chat; review stadiums submitted by Owners; handle reports and abuse; detect and resolve technical issues; maintain the security of the App; respond to support requests; maintain records and obligations related to operating the service; and handle legal requests or proceedings that reach us.

We do not sell users' personal data, we do not use it for behavioral advertising, and we do not place advertising trackers in the App.

6. Sharing Data

  • Stadium Owners: When you book a stadium, the Owner may receive the information needed to fulfill the booking and host you, such as your name, contact number, booking time, and number of players.
  • Other Users: Participants in a match or chat may be able to see your username and profile information made available to them within the App.
  • Service Providers: We rely on third-party providers, including Amazon Web Services (AWS) for hosting, Supabase for database and file storage, Resend for email delivery, Google for maps and geocoding, and push notification services (Expo and web push) for delivering notifications. Where card or wallet payments are enabled in the future, a third-party payment provider will process them.
  • Advisors and Authorities: We may share data when necessary to handle a dispute, claim, or legal proceeding, or to protect the rights of users or the safety of the service.
  • Business Transfers: If the App is sold, or the business is merged or transferred to another party, certain data may be transferred to the party that takes over its operation, with appropriate steps taken to notify users where required.

7. Transfer of Data Outside Egypt

Some data may be processed outside Egypt because our service providers operate in other countries. These may include countries in the European Union and the United States. We choose service providers that apply appropriate data-protection measures. Processing locations may change as our infrastructure or providers change.

8. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • Account and profile data: kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize the data described in Section 11.
  • Booking, payment, and financial records: kept longer where needed for financial records, disputes, or legal obligations.
  • Chat messages and reports: kept as needed to operate the service and to address safety issues, disputes, or reports.
  • Verification data, push tokens, and server logs: kept as needed for security, troubleshooting, and operating the service.

We do not set a fixed retention period for every category. We keep data only while there is a legitimate need or a legal obligation, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

9. Data Security

  • Encrypting communication between the App and our servers using TLS.
  • Storing passwords as salted hashes.
  • Keeping session tokens in secure storage on the device.
  • Restricting access to production data to authorized persons.
  • Reviewing security measures as needed.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security of data. If we discover a security incident affecting data, we will take appropriate steps to address it and notify affected parties or the competent authorities where required.

10. Your Rights

You may contact us about your personal data to request:

  • To know what data is associated with your account, and to obtain a copy of it.
  • To correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • To delete your account and associated data, subject to the records we may need to retain (see Section 11).
  • To restrict or object to certain processing.
  • To withdraw any consent you have given, where possible.
  • To receive your data in a commonly used format where technically feasible.

You can edit some of your profile data and cancel bookings directly within the App. For other requests, including account deletion, email e7gzsupport@gmail.com. We will review the request and handle it within a reasonable period. If you believe your rights were violated, you may lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority in Egypt.

11. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  • From inside the App, from the account settings; or
  • Through our account-deletion page at https://app.e7gz.app/delete-account; or
  • By emailing e7gzsupport@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account.

When your account is deleted:

  • We remove your sign-in details (email, username, and password) and anonymize your profile so it no longer identifies you.
  • We delete your active sessions, push tokens, notifications, favorites, blocked-users list, and profile photo.
  • We keep some records where we have a legal or legitimate reason — for example, booking and payment records, completed matches and their player lists, reviews, chat messages, reports, and uploaded payment-proof images. These are retained as described in Section 8. Some of these records may still contain details you provided, such as a name shown on a receipt.

If you want a record erased that we are keeping, email us and we will assess the request against our legal obligations.

12. Children

The App is intended for people aged 18 or older. When registering, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. We do not aim to collect data from people under 18. If we learn that a person under this age has created an account, you can contact us at e7gzsupport@gmail.com.

13. Device Permissions and Local Storage

  • Location: To show nearby stadiums and directions. Optional; can be controlled from your device settings.
  • Photos/Media: To select profile pictures, stadium photos, or a payment screenshot. Optional.
  • Notifications: To inform you about bookings, matches, and payments. Optional; can be controlled from your device settings.

We may also store your language, theme, and authentication token on your device. The authentication token is used to recognize your session when connecting to our servers.

14. Third-Party Services

The App may use services provided by third parties, such as maps, infrastructure, hosting, email, notifications, and payment services. When these services are used, some data may be processed by their providers in accordance with their own policies and terms.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as a result of changes to the App, the services, or how we handle data. When we make material changes, we will update the version or effective date, and we may notify you through the App or other appropriate means.

16. Contact

For any questions or requests related to privacy and personal data: e7gzsupport@gmail.com
